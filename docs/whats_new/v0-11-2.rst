v0.11.2 - Rankine's Renaissance (September, 3, 2026)
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

New Features
############
- The EN 12900 fitting and efficiency map generation for the
  :py:class:`~tespy.components.displacementmachinery.polynomial_compressor.PolynomialCompressor`
  support non-rectangular data: :code:`nan` cells in datasheet tables are
  excluded from the fits. The methods
  :py:func:`~tespy.components.displacementmachinery.polynomial_compressor.fit_EN12900_points` and
  :py:func:`~tespy.components.displacementmachinery.polynomial_compressor.generate_eta_polys_from_points`
  accept flattened arrays of data points directly
  (`PR #1056 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1056>`__).

Bug Fixes
#########
- The starting value generation split the total mass balance of combustion
  components arbitrarily, since the stoichiometry and the energy balance cannot
  enter the presolve on a guessed fluid composition. The
  :py:class:`~tespy.components.combustion.base.CombustionChamber` and its
  subclasses now contribute an approximate air to fuel proportion - based on the
  oxygen supply and demand of the inlets and on :code:`lamb`, where it is
  specified - to the flow presolve, which leads to more reasonable initial
  guesses for the individual mass flows
  (`PR #1058 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1058>`__).

Other Changes
#############
- Three new example models are available in the
  :ref:`Model Library <model_library_label>`
  (`PR #1059 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1059>`__):

  - A :ref:`water-to-water heat pump <model_heat_pump_partload_label>` with an
    internal heat exchanger and EN 12900 compressor maps, including the
    offdesign evaluation of its part load behavior.
  - A :ref:`supercritical CO2 recompression cycle <model_sco2_label>`, a 100 MW
    Brayton cycle validated against published data.
  - A :ref:`combined cycle power plant <model_ccpp_label>` of 300 MW with
    district heating extraction.

- The documentation has been restructured into the main sections "Get started",
  "Explore", "Build", "Project" and "Reference". The former advanced features,
  integration and knowledge center pages are now split into the
  :ref:`how-to guides <how_to_guides_label>`, :ref:`extend <extend_label>` and
  :ref:`integrate <integrate_label>` sections and the complete simulation
  models moved from the tutorials to the
  :ref:`Model Library <model_library_label>`
  (`PR #1059 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1059>`__).
- The new :ref:`publications page <publications_label>` lists the works using
  or citing tespy. If your work is missing, please open an issue or a pull
  request (`PR #1059 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1059>`__).
