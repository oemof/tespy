v0.12.0 - Under development
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

API Changes
###########
- The data containers of components and connections have been restructured
  with clearly separated responsibilities: every container stores one kind of
  value and either carries an equation or not. The former names stay
  available for one release with a :code:`FutureWarning` and still match
  their replacement in :code:`isinstance` checks
  (`PR #1097 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1097>`__).

  .. list-table::
      :header-rows: 1
      :widths: 30 30 40

      * - Former container
        - New container
        - Role
      * - :code:`FluidProperties`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ConnectionParameter`
        - scalar connection parameter, may be a variable or carry an equation
      * - :code:`ComponentProperties`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ComponentParameter`
        - scalar component parameter, may be a variable or carry an equation
      * - :code:`ComponentArrayProperties`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ArrayParameter`
        - array valued component parameter, input or result
      * - :code:`SimpleDataContainer`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ConfigParameter`
        - typed configuration value without unit and equation
      * - :code:`SimpleDataContainer` with equation,
          :code:`GroupedComponentCharacteristics`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.OptionalEquation`
        - equation switched on by the user, e.g. :code:`cone`,
          :code:`fluid_balance`, :code:`UA_char`
      * - :code:`GroupedComponentProperties`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ParameterGroup`
        - equation activated when all element parameters are specified
      * - :code:`ComponentCharacteristics`,
          :code:`ComponentCharacteristicMaps` without equation
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.CharacteristicData`
        - characteristic read by another equation
      * - :code:`ComponentCharacteristics`,
          :code:`ComponentCharacteristicMaps` with equation
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.CharacteristicEquation`
        - characteristic with its own equation
      * - :code:`ComponentMandatoryConstraints`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.MandatoryConstraint`
        - always active equation of a component
      * - :code:`ReferencedFluidProperties`
        - :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ReferenceEquation`
        - reference of a connection parameter to another connection

  Characteristic containers accept any characteristic object with the
  declared number of inputs. Providing a characteristic to a data container
  marks it as specified, e.g. for the activation of a parameter group,
  providing one to an equation container does not activate the equation.

- Characteristic functions carry a physical quantity per axis. Components
  evaluate them through the container with SI inputs and receive SI outputs,
  the conversion to the units of the data is handled by the container.
  Dimensioned axes follow the default units of the network like any other
  specification. Before, these data were SI data regardless of the network's
  units. This changes the interpretation of user data for the pump's
  :code:`flow_char` (volumetric flow, pressure difference),
  :code:`eta_flow_map` and :code:`head_flow_map` (frequency, volumetric
  flow, head) and the valve's :code:`dp_char` (mass flow, pressure
  difference). Relative characteristics, e.g. :code:`eta_s_char`, are
  dimensionless and unaffected. A warning is raised where the interpretation
  changed, i.e. for dimensioned data without declared units in a network
  whose defaults are not SI. Check your data or declare their units on the data,
  :code:`CharLine(x, y, x_unit="m3/s", y_unit="Pa")`, or pass :code:`pint`
  quantity arrays :code:`CharLine(x=Q_(x, "m3/s"), y=Q_(y, "Pa"))`. The
  :code:`EN12900Polynomial` carries the °C of its axes, the default
  characteristics shipped with tespy are SI data.
- Equations of custom components should call :code:`self.my_char.evaluate(x)`
  instead of :code:`self.my_char.char_func.evaluate(x)`.
- The new :py:class:`~tespy.tools.characteristics.Polynomial` and
  :py:class:`~tespy.tools.characteristics.EN12900Polynomial` are
  characteristic representations next to :code:`CharLine` and
  :code:`CharMap`. The :py:class:`~tespy.components.displacementmachinery.polynomial_compressor.PolynomialCompressor`
  takes its efficiency polynomials :code:`eta_s_poly` and :code:`eta_vol_poly`
  as :code:`EN12900Polynomial` objects, which are evaluated in °C as defined by
  the standard whatever the network's temperature unit is. Passing the
  coefficients as list is deprecated.
- The :code:`reference_state` dictionary of the :code:`PolynomialCompressor` is
  deprecated. The component only needs the :code:`swept_volume` (quantity
  :code:`volume`) from it, which is a regular component parameter now. The
  functions generating the efficiency polynomials from manufacturer data take
  the reference state as the keyword arguments :code:`T_sh`, :code:`T_sc`,
  :code:`frequency` and :code:`swept_volume`, the dictionary is deprecated
  there as well. With the keyword :code:`units` the functions read all plain
  numbers, the table and the reference state, in the given unit system, e.g.
  the one of the network the polynomials are used in. Create the unit system
  first, fit, then create the network with :code:`Network(units=units)`.
  Without :code:`units` the table temperatures are °C as defined by EN 12900
  and all other values SI. Quantities carry their own unit in both cases. The
  :code:`EN12900Polynomial` carries the °C of its axes, so the containers
  read them without a declaration.
- A parameter accepts a specification when a declared consumer reads it: an
  equation of its own, a parameter group, optional equation or mandatory
  constraint listing it in its :code:`elements`, the variable role or the state
  state of a connection. A parameter without consumer is result-only and
  refuses user values with a :code:`TypeError` instead of silently
  ignoring them (:issue:`1070`).
- References of connection parameters to the same parameter of another
  connection are attached to the parameter: :code:`c.m.reference` holds the
  :py:class:`~tespy.tools.data_containers.ReferenceEquation`. The former
  sibling parameters :code:`m_ref`, :code:`p_ref`, :code:`h_ref`,
  :code:`T_ref` and :code:`v_ref` are gone from the parameter tables, the
  attributes stay available with a :code:`FutureWarning`.

Other Changes
#############

- Components can declare the role of their ports in
  :py:meth:`~tespy.components.component.Component.port_descriptions`, e.g. the
  hot and cold side of a heat exchanger or the fuel, air and cooling water
  ports of the combustion engine.
- The unit system knows the quantity :code:`volume` with default unit
  :code:`m3`.
- The energy flow :code:`E` of power and heat connections can be referenced
  now, e.g. :code:`e2.set_attr(E=Ref(e1, 0.5, 0))`.
- :code:`UA`, :code:`kA` and :code:`lmtd` of the
  :py:class:`~tespy.components.heat_exchangers.simple.SimpleHeatExchanger`
  and :code:`Q_loss` of the solar collectors are calculated in every case,
  the value is :code:`nan` when the ambient temperature or the energy group
  is not specified. :code:`lmtd` and :code:`Q_loss` are result-only.

Bug Fixes
#########
- The dew and bubble temperature specifications :code:`T_dew` and
  :code:`T_bubble` of connections were silently ignored where the pressure is
  fixed otherwise, e.g. through the pressure equality of a component. This is
  an overdetermination error now. With a variable fluid composition the
  saturation properties are not available: specifying :code:`T_dew`,
  :code:`T_bubble`, :code:`x`, :code:`td_dew` or :code:`td_bubble` there raises
  an error instead of the specification being ignored.
