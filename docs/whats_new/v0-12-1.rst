v0.12.1 - Under development
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

New Features
############
- The block decomposition and the structural analysis cover networks with
  variable fluid composition. Every equation listing the pressure or the
  enthalpy of a connection is assumed to depend on the variable mass fractions
  of that connection as well, so the dependence of fluid property equations on
  the composition no longer has to be declared. Block-wise solving of such
  networks is available with
  :code:`nw.solve(mode, block_solve_fluid_composition=True)`, the default
  remains the simultaneous solution for now (`#1075 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1075>`__).
- Equations that list pressure or enthalpy without evaluating a fluid property
  can switch the inference off with :code:`infer_composition=False`, both in
  the parameter and constraint definitions of components and in the
  :code:`UserDefinedEquation`. The energy balances and heat and power
  equations of the core components written in mass flow and enthalpy do so
  (`#1075 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1075>`__).
- :py:meth:`~tespy.networks.network.Network.get_dependents_check` and
  :py:meth:`~tespy.networks.network.Network.print_dependents_check` compare
  the dependents of the equations with their observed dependence by
  perturbing every variable once. Use it to check the dependents of custom
  components and user defined equations
  (`#1075 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1075>`__).

Bug Fixes
#########
- :code:`Network.solve` could hang forever without any output in the flow
  presolve of the starting values. On nearly singular systems, the bounded
  least squares of :code:`scipy.optimize.lsq_linear` with its default TRF
  method never returned, because its backtracking line search shrank the step
  length to zero without accepting a step. The flow presolve now uses the BVLS
  method, which has a bounded number of iterations.
