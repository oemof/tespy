v0.12.1 - Under development
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

New Features
############
- Absorption cycles: the :code:`SolutionConnection` carries the streams of a
  working pair, the components :code:`Absorber`, :code:`CooledAbsorber`,
  :code:`Desorber`, :code:`HeatedDesorber` and :code:`Rectifier` model the
  sorption processes. The working pairs LiBr-water (Pátek and Klomfar 2006)
  and NH3-water (IAPWS G4-01 on the kernel of the optional dependency teqp)
  are available, users can register their own pairs. See
  :ref:`tutorial_absorption_cycles_label` (:issue:`1016`).
- Mixing rules can provide functions for the temperature from enthalpy and
  entropy, the phase, the vapor mass fraction and the bubble and dew points
  of a mixture. The specifications :code:`x`, :code:`td_bubble` and
  :code:`td_dew` then work on mixtures as well.

Bug Fixes
#########
- :code:`Network.solve` could hang forever without any output in the flow
  presolve of the starting values. On nearly singular systems, the bounded
  least squares of :code:`scipy.optimize.lsq_linear` with its default TRF
  method never returned, because its backtracking line search shrank the step
  length to zero without accepting a step. The flow presolve now uses the BVLS
  method, which has a bounded number of iterations.
