Bug Fixes
#########
- :code:`Network.solve` could hang forever without any output in the flow
  presolve of the starting values. On nearly singular systems, the bounded
  least squares of :code:`scipy.optimize.lsq_linear` with its default TRF
  method never returned, because its backtracking line search shrank the step
  length to zero without accepting a step. The flow presolve now uses the BVLS
  method, which has a bounded number of iterations
  (`PR #1126 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1126>`__).
- Enthalpy and entropy of the :code:`IncompressibleFluidWrapper` are now
  thermodynamically consistent: the entropy had a wrong pressure term and the
  enthalpy none. Results change at pressures other than the reference
  pressure (default 1 bar), e.g. entropy, exergy and isentropic states
  (`PR #1129 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1129>`__).
- A :code:`Pump` with the :code:`IncompressibleFluidWrapper` failed with a
  :code:`NotImplementedError`, because it requested saturation states of a
  fluid without saturation
  (`PR #1129 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1129>`__).
