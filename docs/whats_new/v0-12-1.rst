v0.12.1 - Under development
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Bug Fixes
#########
- :code:`Network.solve` could hang forever without any output in the flow
  presolve of the starting values. On nearly singular systems, the bounded
  least squares of :code:`scipy.optimize.lsq_linear` with its default TRF
  method never returned, because its backtracking line search shrank the step
  length to zero without accepting a step. The flow presolve now uses the BVLS
  method, which has a bounded number of iterations.
- The entropy of the :code:`IncompressibleFluidWrapper` had a wrong pressure
  term (density times pressure difference). It now follows the Maxwell
  relation :math:`\left(\partial s / \partial p\right)_T = \left(\partial \rho
  / \partial T\right)_p / \rho^2`. Entropy and exergy results of fluids
  using this wrapper change at pressures other than the reference pressure
  (default 1 bar), and the inversion of temperature from entropy no longer
  fails across different pressures (`Issue #1127
  <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1127>`__).
- The isentropic enthalpy of mixtures with the :code:`"incompressible"`
  mixing rule is now the inlet specific volume times the pressure change,
  in the same way as for a single incompressible fluid. Before, it was
  calculated via entropy, which is inconsistent for fluids with temperature
  dependent enthalpy only.

Other Changes
#############
- The test data of the :code:`IncompressibleFluidWrapper` now use the
  ISTpure Standard Heat Transfer Oil datasheet. The previous heat capacity
  data were off by a factor of 1000.
