v0.12.2 - Under development
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++

New Features
############
- The new :ref:`plotting module <plotting_label>` draws property diagrams
  (with CoolPlot), Q-T diagrams, characteristics, results per component,
  parameter sweeps, operating maps and the convergence history, each with
  matplotlib and with Plotly. Plotly plots show the values behind every point
  when hovered. All plots share the styles :code:`"tespy"`,
  :code:`"tespy-dark"` and :code:`"tespy-paper"`, users can register their
  own, and show the TESPy logo with an information block. The data of every
  plot can be exported to JSON. Install the dependencies with
  :code:`pip install tespy[plot]` or :code:`tespy[interactive]`
  (`Issue #1130 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1130>`__).

Deprecations
############
- The fluprodia based plotting of the model template is deprecated and will
  be removed in version 0.14
  (`Issue #1130 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/issues/1130>`__):

  .. list-table::
      :header-rows: 1

      * - Deprecated
        - New
      * - :code:`plot_logph_diagram_matplotlib(label)`
        - :code:`plot_property_diagram(label, "ph")`
      * - :code:`plot_Ts_diagram_matplotlib(label)`
        - :code:`plot_property_diagram(label, "Ts")`
      * - :code:`plot_QT_diagram_matplotlib(label)`
        - :code:`plot_QT_diagram(label)`
      * - :code:`register_diagram(fluid, diagram)`
        - :code:`diagram_hook` of :code:`plot_property_diagram`
      * - :code:`CharLine.plot`, :code:`CharMap.plot`
        - :code:`tespy.plotting.plot_characteristic`

Bug Fixes
#########
- :code:`Pipe` evaluated Prandtl number and thermal conductivity of the ambient
  air at the last state of the CoolProp backend instead of at ambient pressure
  and temperature. The :code:`CoolPropWrapper` now provides the Prandtl number
  from pressure and temperature
  (`PR #1136 <https://github.com/oemof/tespy/pull/1136>`__).
- A custom fuel registered for use in :code:`CombustionChamber` was not
  recognized a under different alias of the same fluid.
- The :code:`SectionedHeatExchanger` raised an :code:`IndexError` in the
  :code:`area_zones` equation when a fluid side with pressure drop enters
  exactly at saturated vapor or leaves exactly at saturated liquid, e.g.
  during a derivative evaluation: the phase boundary steps kept the degenerate
  boundary while the zone phases did not.

Documentation
#############
- The documentation on mixtures with the CoolProp :code:`HEOS` back end is
  updated: CoolProp version 8.1 or higher is recommended for these, the
  remaining limitations are listed and the
  :ref:`comparison of the back ends <fluid_property_backends_label>` is
  rerun with that version.
